How to run this test

  1. In the form's Settings → Privacy tab, set Ad & conversion tracking (and/or Surface analytics) to On consent.
  2. Give the form something to block: a GTM container, GA4 measurement ID, Meta Pixel, or HubSpot tracking ID on the other settings tabs. Surface analytics needs nothing configured.
  3. Point Form URL above at that form and reload. Open DevTools → Network and filter on:
    googletagmanager|google-analytics|connect.facebook|facebook.com/tr|hs-scripts|posthog|vercel-scripts
  4. Before answering the banner: no requests match. That is the whole point — the form rendered, but nothing third-party loaded.
  5. Accept in the banner: the matching vendor requests appear within a second, with no page reload. Reject instead and the filter stays empty.

Withdrawing consent after accepting stops further calls but cannot unload scripts that already started — reload the page to get back to a clean slate.
